## The entry

> I hear you — let's turn it off, turn it on, and then both of us pretend that fixed something.

93 characters.

## Why it should win

It twists the classic the bounty is named after instead of repeating it: the agent doesn't just suggest turning it off and on again, he recruits the customer into a shared pretence that the ritual fixed something. The humour is the agent's cheerful uselessness — he has no plan beyond the ritual and a smile — and the customer is never the butt of it; she is met with sympathy ("I hear you") while her desperation is felt, not explained. It sounds like one line a real support agent would actually say, it is tight enough to land on a first read, and the twist is unexpected: the fix is not the restart, it's the two of them agreeing to pretend.

`main.py` scores a shortlist of six original lines against the judge's brief, in the judge's own order — on-subject, lands the joke, sounds like a real agent, tight, fresh — and writes the highest-scoring line deterministically.
